Overview

Three core units,
one autonomous coating job

RZ Titan is purpose-built for coating ship interiors. It combines three units: a mobile platform, a spraying unit, and a perception-and-control unit.

  • Mobile platform unit

    An eight-leg telescopic structure walks and steps over the complex terrain inside compartments.

  • Spraying unit

    A multi-axis, explosion-proof spray arm precisely controls coating quality.

  • Perception & control unit

    Integrated vision scanning, path planning and film inspection enable fully autonomous work.

Rib-crossing gait

Meet a rib frame.
Step right over it.

On open floor it rolls on wheels. At a rib frame, the front and rear leg groups take turns to lift, cross and land, then the robot rolls on.

Mobility

Dual-deck, eight-leg telescopic mechanism

Our original dual-deck mechanism, with eight telescopic legs in four groups, crosses 600 mm rib frames, 3× the industry norm, and solves the problem of moving through ribbed compartments.

  • Obstacle height ≤ 600 mm
  • Grade ≤ 10°
  • Travel speed 500 mm/s

Chassis

Four steering wheels, same-side and diagonal

The steering wheels sit on the same side and across the diagonal, so the robot can move sideways or spin in place to reposition inside narrow compartments.

Spraying

Cartesian gantry + explosion-proof arm

A Cartesian robot carries the nozzle to every work area, and a multi-axis explosion-proof spray arm keeps coating quality under precise control across a 0–⁠3.2 m working height.

  • Working height 0–⁠3.2 m
  • Full coverage, no blind spots
  • Intrinsically safe design

Perception & control

From drawings to finished coat, fully autonomous

RZ Radar scans the compartment on site (or you import the drawings), the scan is registered to the hull CAD, and the spray path is generated automatically. Loaded into our in-house Ranzhou System, the robot carries out the job end to end, while vision recognition and online film inspection close the quality loop.

  • On-site scan by RZ Radar
  • Registered to the hull CAD
  • Online film inspection

Workflow

One autonomous job, four steps

  1. 01

    Scan & model

    Scan the compartment on site with RZ Radar, or import the drawings.

  2. 02

    Plan the path

    The spray path is generated automatically.

  3. 03

    Load the plan

    The plan is loaded into our in-house Ranzhou System.

  4. 04

    Coat autonomously

    The robot coats the space end to end and inspects the film online.
