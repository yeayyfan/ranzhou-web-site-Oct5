Role

Survey, plan and navigate
from one station.

RZ Radar goes into the tank before coating starts. It unfolds three legs, raises its mast and scans, producing a 3D model registered to the hull CAD along with the spray paths. Once spraying begins, it stays in the tank as the positioning base station for RZ Titan and RZ Spider.

  • Lidar scan head

    A fast-spinning lidar mirror drum with a 360° × 300° field of view, inside a guard frame that doubles as a handle.

  • 4D mmWave radar ring + 4 HDR cameras

    The radars see through paint mist to track robots and people, and the cameras color the point cloud.

  • Two-stage telescopic mast

    400 mm of travel lifts the scan center to 1,382 mm.

  • Core

    Jetson AGX Orin compute, two hot-swappable 98 Wh batteries, and temperature, humidity, dew-point and LEL sensors.

  • Three self-leveling legs

    Carbon-fiber legs deploy electrically, with telescoping lower sections and ±12° of leveling. Switchable permanent-magnet pads lock onto the steel floor.

Deploy

Carried in by one person,
unfolded in the tank

Stowed, it stands 842 mm tall and 256 mm across and weighs about 14 kg, so one person can carry it through a 600 × 800 mm manhole. On site, three legs swing out, land and self-level, the magnetic pads lock, and the two-stage mast rises to put the scan center up to 1,382 mm high.

  • Stowed: 842 × Ø256 mm
  • About 14 kg
  • Deployed height 1,092–⁠1,492 mm

Scan & model

Lidar, radar and cameras
capture every structure

The lidar mirror drum covers a 360° × 300° field of view, four HDR cameras color the point cloud in full panorama, and scans from several stations are stitched automatically into a complete 3D model of the compartment.

Register & plan

Registered to the hull CAD,
turned into spray paths

Once the point cloud is registered to the hull CAD, the system outputs as-built deviations, the area to coat, spray paths and a navigation map for RZ Titan and RZ Spider to follow.

  • As-built deviations
  • Coating area and spray paths
  • Navigation map

Base station

It stays in the tank
to guide the robots

4D mmWave radar sees through paint mist to keep tracking robots and people in the tank. UWB provides positioning, and a Wi-Fi mesh relays through the manholes. It also monitors temperature, humidity, dew point, steel temperature and flammable gas (LEL), supplying the environmental data for coating records.

  • Tracks through paint mist
  • UWB positioning · Wi-Fi mesh
  • Temperature · humidity · dew point · LEL
