Leave the hazardous,
repetitive coating to robots.

The RZ series serves shipbuilders and repair yards. RZ Radar surveys the tank first, RZ Titan crosses rib frames to coat large areas, and RZ Spider climbs the walls and passes through manholes into the tightest compartments.

Our robots

Redefining how
work gets done in the hull

Robots that move through three-dimensional space extend the work envelope to bulkheads, the gaps between rib frames, and tight enclosed spaces.

  1. Reach the whole tank

    Wheels and legs cross rib frames, and magnetic feet climb walls and pass through manholes, reaching places people struggle to get to.

  2. Integrated workflow

    Survey, path planning, spraying and quality records all happen in one system.

  3. Autonomy & safety

    Autonomous operation keeps people out of enclosed tanks, while intrinsically safe design and magnets that hold without power lower operating risk.

01

RZ Titan

Autonomous interior-coating robot for ship compartments

A dual-deck, eight-leg telescopic platform crosses 600 mm rib frames. An intrinsically safe spray arm covers 0–⁠3.2 m, and vision recognition with online film inspection closes the loop, from path planning to the finished coat.

Details
02

RZ Spider

Magnetic legged robot for work inside the hull

Four electro-permanent magnetic feet carry it from the tank floor up the walls. At a manhole the body goes through first and the legs follow one by one, with at least three feet attached throughout. Sealed joints and body make it explosion-proof by design, and its six-axis arm swaps end tools to spray, weld and inspect.

Details
03

RZ Radar

Hold survey & navigation station

Before coating starts, it scans the compartment and registers the scan to the hull CAD to produce spray paths and a navigation map. Once spraying begins, it stays in the tank as the positioning base station for RZ Titan and RZ Spider, logging temperature, humidity and dew point.

Details

How they work together

One system,
from survey to coating

RZ Radar handles survey and navigation, RZ Titan and RZ Spider take the floor and the walls, and data runs through every step.

  1. 01

    Survey

    One person carries RZ Radar into the tank, where it unfolds, scans and registers to the hull CAD.

    RZ Radar

  2. 02

    Plan

    Spray paths, a navigation map and work zones are generated and loaded into our in-house Ranzhou System.

  3. 03

    Work

    RZ Titan crosses rib frames and coats from 0 to 3.2 m in open sections; RZ Spider climbs the walls and passes through manholes into tight compartments.

    RZ TitanRZ Spider

  4. 04

    Track & record

    RZ Radar stays in the tank to track robots and people and log temperature, humidity and dew point, while the robots report film thickness for the coating quality record.

    RZ Radar
